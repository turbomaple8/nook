Applying for 2027 Grad Schemes? Sort the London Question Early

You're polishing cover letters. Future-you is wondering where to live. Both are right.

Grad scheme season runs on a long fuse: applications this autumn, assessment centres through winter, offers in spring, start date next September. Somewhere inside that year-long arc sits a housing decision most applicants don't think about until August — and then think about in a panic. Here's what's actually worth doing at each stage, starting now.

Now (Application Season): Know Your Number

You can't sensibly compare offers without knowing what London costs. The one-evening exercise: a typical first-year salary of £28–38k means take-home of £1,900–£2,400/month; a workable budget puts rent-with-bills at 40–45% of that. Run it once and two things happen — salary negotiations get real, and the "London vs regional office" choice some schemes offer becomes a maths question instead of a vibes question. The first-quarter budget is the template.

Offer Stage (Winter–Spring): Research, Don't Book

Offer signed, start date confirmed — the housing instinct says "book something now". Resist it: booking a September room in March means paying for certainty you don't need yet (rotations move, locations change). What is worth doing now: pick two or three target neighbourhoods by commute (the first-jobber map), understand the market's standard terms — a 12-month minimum, a four-week deposit held in a protection scheme — and bookmark the providers whose contracts and prices are published rather than quoted.

Summer: The Six-Week Runway

The real booking window is six to eight weeks out, and the full month-by-month plan already exists — the grad scheme housing timeline covers August's lock-the-essentials sequence, the paperwork (Right to Rent documents, UK bank account), and the one mistake to avoid: panic-signing anything in week one of the job.

The Decision That's Actually Strategic

Beyond postcode and price sits a quieter choice: what kind of household do you want your first London year to happen in? Grad schemes are socially front-loaded — cohort events, training weeks — but the flat is where the city becomes home. A shared flat of people at the same life stage converts the hardest part of relocating (the adult friendship problem) into a default feature. It's the part of the housing decision worth deciding early, because it shapes everything the spreadsheet can't measure.

2027 cohort: applications now, number-crunching this month, booking next summer.
