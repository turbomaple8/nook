Intern Cost of Living in London: A Realistic Weekly Budget

Your stipend covers London — if you know where the money actually goes each week.

Internship budgeting has a specific shape: a fixed stipend, a fixed number of weeks, and a city with strong opinions about your wallet. Monthly budgets blur it; weekly is how intern money actually moves. Here's the line-by-line week, at 2026 prices.

The Weekly Ledger

Rent: £215–£320/week. The dominant line. The critical distinction is all-in versus rent-plus-bills: an all-inclusive room from £215/week (subject to room availability) is a known number; a "£200" room plus energy, WiFi and council tax is a £230–£250 number wearing a disguise. For a 10-week stay you also need a contract that fits — our intern housing guide covers the short-stay options.

Transport: £40–£48/week. Zones 1-2 pay-as-you-go caps at about £45/week. Live within walking or cycling distance of the office and this line collapses to near zero — the strongest argument for choosing housing by commute (the 20-minute map).

Food: £55–£90/week. The intern trap is lunch: £8–£12 daily near any office district is £50/week on its own. The fix is boring and works — cook four evenings, bring lunch three days, keep two social meals guilt-free. Shared kitchens make batch-cooking with flatmates both cheaper and more fun.

Social: £30–£60/week. Pints are £6.50–£8, cinema £12–£15 — but London's best intern currency is free: parks, markets, galleries, and the office social calendar someone else is paying for. Say yes to those first.

The sneaky extras: £10–£20/week. Laundry (if not in-flat), phone plan, toiletries, the occasional umbrella you'll lose. Small lines, real money.

Three Weekly Totals

Lean week: ~£360 — all-in room at entry price, walk-to-work, disciplined lunches. Normal week: ~£430 — Zone 2 room, travel cap, balanced social life. First week: add £100+ — setup costs front-load; our first-quarter budget maps that curve.

Making the Stipend Fit

A typical London intern stipend lands £480–£600/week gross. Against a normal £430 week that's tight but workable — and the levers are ranked: housing choice moves £50–£100/week, lunch discipline £30–£40, transport-by-location up to £45. Nothing else comes close, so optimise in that order. One upfront number to plan for: the deposit — four weeks' rent (one full rent period) at Nook, protected in a government scheme, back at the end.
