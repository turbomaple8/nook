Council Tax for Students in London: Exemptions, Explained

The tax most students never have to pay — and the letter that arrives anyway.

Council tax is the bill that confuses every new arrival: a local charge on the household, typically £1,700–£2,100 a year for a London flat, that most other countries simply don't have. The good news for students is simple — full-time students are usually exempt. The catch: the exemption doesn't apply itself. Here's how it actually works, and the ten-minute September job that stops scary letters arriving in November.

Who Counts as Exempt

You're disregarded for council tax if you're on a full-time course — broadly, at least one year long and 21+ hours of study a week. Undergraduates and full-time postgraduates qualify; part-time students generally don't. A household where everyone is a full-time student pays nothing at all.

The Mixed-Household Rules (Where It Gets Interesting)

All students: fully exempt — but the council doesn't know until someone tells them.

Students + one working adult: the property gets a bill with a 25% single-person-style discount — and here's the friction: legally the bill attaches to the non-student. If you're the one worker in a student flat, that's worth knowing before you sign; if you're the students, it's worth being kind about. We flagged this dynamic in the flatmate questions guide.

Students + two or more workers: full bill for the household, no discount. The workers split it; students still can't be chased for it.

The September To-Do: Get the Certificate In

Three steps, ten minutes: 1) Download your student status certificate from your university portal (usually under "documents" or via student services) once you've enrolled. 2) Find your borough council's "student council tax exemption" form — every London borough has one online. 3) Upload the certificate for each student in the flat. Done. If a bill arrives before processing completes, don't panic and don't ignore it — reply referencing your application; councils pause collection while they check.

The Version Where None of This Is Your Problem

In all-inclusive housing, council tax sits inside the weekly price whoever you are — student, intern or working professional. At Nook it's bundled with WiFi, energy and water in one number from £215/week (subject to room availability), so the mixed-household maths above simply never lands on your doormat. The full inclusions breakdown is in the bills-included guide.

Quick Answers

Do international students qualify? Yes — exemption is about full-time study, not nationality. Does a placement year count? Usually yes if it's part of your course; confirm with your university, as boroughs ask for course dates. Summer between years? If your course spans the summer (enrolment continues), the exemption holds. Just graduated? The exemption ends with your course — budget for council tax from that month in your first-salary budget.
